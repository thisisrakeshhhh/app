# RouteFlow — FMCG Analytics, KPIs & Business Intelligence Metrics
**Domain:** FMCG Distribution Intelligence  
**Target Personas:** Owner, Sales TL, Godown Manager, Logistics Head  

---

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 1.1 Sales Force Effectiveness (SFA)
1. **Total Calls vs. Productive Calls**:
   $$\text{Productivity Ratio} = \frac{\text{Shops with Booked Orders}}{\text{Total Shops Visited on Beat}} \times 100$$
   *Industry Target: > 75% productive calls per beat day.*
2. **Beat Adherence / Route Compliance**:
   $$\text{Beat Adherence} = \frac{\text{Assigned Beat Shops Visited}}{\text{Total Assigned Shops on Beat}} \times 100$$
3. **Lines Per Call (LPC)**:
   $$\text{LPC} = \frac{\text{Total Distinct SKUs Sold Across Orders}}{\text{Total Productive Orders}}$$
   *Measures cross-selling and brand basket penetration.*

### 1.2 Supply Chain & Inventory Health
1. **Order Fulfillment Rate (OTIF - On-Time In-Full)**:
   $$\text{OTIF} = \frac{\text{Orders Delivered on Schedule with Zero Line Cuts}}{\text{Total Approved Orders}} \times 100$$
2. **Batch Expiry Risk Ratio**:
   $$\text{Stock at Risk} = \frac{\text{Value of Batches Expiring within 60 Days}}{\text{Total Warehouse Inventory Value}} \times 100$$
3. **Inventory Turnover Ratio (ITR)**:
   $$\text{ITR} = \frac{\text{Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)}}{\text{Average Inventory Value at Cost}}$$

### 1.3 Financial & Credit Health
1. **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)**:
   $$\text{DSO} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Accounts Receivable / Khata Balance}}{\text{Gross Credit Sales over Period}} \right) \times \text{Days in Period}$$
2. **Collection Efficiency**:
   $$\text{Collection Efficiency} = \frac{\text{Actual Cash/UPI/Cheque Collected}}{\text{Total Due / Collectible Amount}} \times 100$$
3. **Gross Margin Percentage by SKU & Category**:
   $$\text{Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Selling Price (Excl. Tax)} - \text{Purchase Cost (Excl. Tax)}}{\text{Selling Price (Excl. Tax)}} \times 100$$
